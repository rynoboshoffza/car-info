Tips

Controls

  • Regen: set the Favourite (star) button to Regen. It reverts to level 2 every time the car is powered off.
  • Cruise and steering assist: on the left scroll wheel, hold to pre-enable or disable steering assist (grey steering wheel icon). Click once to pre-engage cruise, then release the accelerator; the cruise icon turns green and the steering icon blue. One owner drove 20 km of the N1 without disengaging. Practise on a quiet road with good lane markings.
  • Following distance (adaptive cruise): roll the left scroll wheel sideways: right for a larger gap, left for smaller. There are 5 levels, default 3, shown as a blue bar on the small HUD. Pressing the roller cancels adaptive cruise, so do not press it.
  • Cruise speed steps: a click changes 5 km/h below 100 km/h and 10 km/h above. 1 km/h steps are disputed (Conflicting info). The car cannot resume the last set speed; it sets the current speed.
  • Lane assist: warnings show an orange line normally. One owner saw a different sound and a red line when another car was approaching or overtaking.
  • Head-up display: the right steering wheel button cycles music, range stats, and tyre pressure and temperature.
  • Windows: long-press the unlock button on the key to lower all windows.
  • Switching off: the car switches off when you lock it with the key. The settings menu has a turn-off option, and the car stays on while you sit in it. The drag-down screen holds more functions.
  • Gear stalk: selecting R while driving does nothing. The stalk is software-controlled and needs the brake held while stationary.
  • Backup key: the loose key opens a keyhole under the driver's door handle (lift the handle). How to start the car with a dead remote is unconfirmed.
  • Shortcuts: the infotainment screen accepts apps and shortcuts, including a tyre-pressure monitor (shown in a member's video).
  • Calls: taking a call through the car lowers the fan speed for its duration.
  • Speedometer: one owner says it is accurate; another car's speedometer showed 120 when the Q showed 110.
  • Outside speaker: sound effects are available (train, whale, sword). One owner asked how to set the outside volume; no answer.

Crash Mode

  • After a light bump, one owner's car showed a Crash Mode alert. CarPlay, steering wheel volume and the electric frunk were disabled, and the frunk had to be opened manually.
  • In a serious crash with airbag deployment, the car stops driving to protect the battery and needs roadside assistance (the same owner's understanding).
  • A dealer service department cleared the alert in about 15 minutes.

Efficiency and performance

  • Best results came with AC off, mild weather and smooth acceleration. Regen level 3 on a long mountain descent followed by 100 km/h without AC gave one owner a very good result.
  • Consumption: 9.9 kWh/100 km (best) to 12.4 kWh/100 km; about 14 kWh/100 km on a mixed 80–100 km/h commute (also with bikes on roof racks); about 16 kWh/100 km at 100 km/h with AC; 17 kWh/100 km average for another owner.
  • Range: two owners arrived with 4% left, one after 289 km and one after starting at 80% with 160 km to go into a headwind.
  • 0–100 km/h: spec about 13 s; measured 10.3 s (unforced), 9.9 s and 9.3 s (Sport, two owners). Strong from 30 to 80 km/h, soft from 0 to 30.
  • Top speed: 144 km/h on the speedometer and GPS at 39% battery (one owner).
  • One owner prefers Eco mode for daily driving. Some Elite owners switched off most ADAS features (one says about 90%).
  • Hill Descent Control has not been tested by owners; one thinks it uses regen braking.
