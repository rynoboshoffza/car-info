Issues and open questions

Reported issues

Camera fails at night No fix

Dealers report it to Chery. A software update is rumoured; no timeframe given.

CarPlay reconnects Workarounds

Manual reconnect for several owners. Others connect automatically, or fixed it by setting the phone as primary device.

Vehicle app Mixed

Works over Bluetooth only for several owners; two owners say it works remotely. One owner emailed the dealer with no reply.

Wall charger app (Tellus Power)

One owner cannot get it to connect and it does not show the bound vehicle. It needs a registration step. Several owners only use the cards.

Driver's seat

One owner reports a sluggish electric seat that does not recline backwards (video).

Charge plug locked in Resolved

Fixed by unlocking the car 2–3 times. See Charging.

Cosmetic

Loose front wheel covers. A windscreen mark that could not be removed. Q-logo stickers in different positions.

Safety and recalls

  • The Q has an LFP battery per owners, considered less fire-prone. A member noted that NMC chemistry is more heat-sensitive and has higher energy density, and that firefighters can mainly cool a lithium fire with water to involve fewer cells while it burns out.
  • Parking an EV in an attached garage was raised as a concern (opinion).
  • The Fireblock lithium extinguisher was praised and called expensive.
  • A safety recall of Chery Omoda vehicles is a different model. Members discussed rear suspension problems on certain models abroad, mostly torsion-beam designs. No Q recall was reported.
