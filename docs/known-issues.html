Issues and open questions

Reported issues

Carlinko app: "no vehicle bound" Partial fix

At least 5 owners could not connect the app, and 3 or more waited 2.5–3 weeks with no help. Dealers' diagnostic tools could not detect one car; one owner suspects the T-box (telematics unit), and another's is being replaced.

On 18 Nov one owner reported a fix: with the car off and unlocked, connect through the Carlinko app over Bluetooth. An update pushes from the phone to the car, after which the app connects over the car's SIM. It worked for that owner; at least 3 others reported no update.

Binding: one dealer bound the wrong car (check the VIN in the app); one owner says it needed the car and phone together, another says it was done remotely using only an email code. A Cape Town dealer said it is waiting for a software update. The app shows the car and charge for one owner but will not open windows. Smash-and-grab film does not affect it (one owner). The infotainment screen shows SIM signal bars at the top left (one owner had never noticed them).

Camera fails at night No fix

Dealers report it to Chery. A software update is rumoured; no timeframe given.

CarPlay reconnects Workarounds

Manual reconnect for several owners. Others connect automatically, or fixed it by setting the phone as primary device at first setup.

Wall charger faults

One new installation does not charge (green port light on the car, steady blue on the box); one owner gets an error updating the firmware. No fixes reported yet. See Charging.

Driver's seat

Two Elite owners report a sluggish electric seat; one says it only reclines on the left side and does not recline fully backwards (video).

Alignment and tyre pressure warnings

See Tyres & wheels.

Cosmetic

Loose front wheel covers. A windscreen mark that could not be removed. Q-logo stickers in different positions. One owner says the plastic cover under the motor is brittle at the back.

Safety and recalls

  • The Q has an LFP battery per owners, considered less fire-prone. A member noted that NMC chemistry is more heat-sensitive and has higher energy density, and that firefighters can mainly cool a lithium fire with water to involve fewer cells while it burns out.
  • Parking an EV in an attached garage was raised as a concern (opinion).
  • A safety recall of Chery Omoda vehicles is a different model. Members discussed rear suspension problems on certain models abroad, mostly torsion-beam designs. No Q recall was reported.
