Chery Q · Issues

Issues & open questions

What's broken, what's unconfirmed, and what owners are still asking. This page is the first place to look when a delta of chat messages answers something.

Reported issues

Camera fails at night

No fix yet Owners say dealers just report it to Chery. A software update is rumoured, but no timeframe was given. Ask your dealer to log it.

CarPlay needs reconnecting

Workarounds Several owners connect manually each time. Others find it eventually auto-connects, or fixed it by setting the phone as the primary device.

Wheel covers sit badly

Cosmetic Comfort/Elite owners report loose or poorly seated front caps. One removed a cover to find plain, unfinished steel wheels behind.

Windscreen mark, sticker alignment

Cosmetic A mark that couldn't be removed; Q-logo stickers placed inconsistently between cars.

Open questions

Asked in the chat without a clear answer. If you know, tell the group so a future update can include it.

  • OpenAndroid Auto: is it supported at all? Asked repeatedly, unanswered.

  • OpenWheel PCD of the 16" 4-bolt wheel ("4x100?").
  • OpenWhich tyre specs count as "EV tyres" and any insurer requirements.
  • OpenWall charger vs spec sheet: spec lists both chargers as included; deliveries say otherwise.

  • OpenLicence-disc holders that work with smash-and-grab film.

Safety talk & recalls

  • A viral Volvo EV fire triggered a lithium-fire discussion. Owners point out the Q uses LFP chemistry, considered safer, though they still debate garage parking. A Fireblock lithium extinguisher was praised but called expensive.
  • Related brand news: a safety recall of Chery Omoda vehicles (a different model; the chat discussed torsion-beam rear suspension problems on certain models abroad, and noted multi-link rear suspension is reported to be less affected).
  • Owners shared a light-hearted note that all brands have recalls; nothing Q-specific was reported.
