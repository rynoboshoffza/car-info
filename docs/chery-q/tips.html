Chery Q · Tips

Tips & hidden features

Small discoveries from owners. Try driver-assist features on a quiet road first.

Regen on the star button

Set the "Favourite" (star) button to Regen for quick access. It reverts to level 2 every time the car is powered off, so you set it each drive.

Cruise + steering assist

Left scroll wheel on the steering wheel: hold to pre-enable/disable steering assist (a grey steering wheel shows). Click once to pre-engage cruise; release the accelerator and it engages (icon turns green, steering icon blue). One owner drove 20 km of the N1 without disengaging. Practise on a quiet road with good lane markings.

Heads-up display

The right-hand steering wheel button cycles the HUD through music, range stats, and tyre pressure/temperature.

Windows down with the key

Long-press the unlock button and all windows go down.

Add shortcuts & apps

The infotainment screen allows apps and shortcuts, with some hidden gestures a member demonstrated in a video; for example a tyre-pressure monitor on the screen.

CarPlay keeps disconnecting?

One owner says the fix was setting the phone as the primary device during the first-time setup. Another found it simply began connecting by itself over time.

Calls lower the fan

With the aircon on full, taking a call through the car automatically drops the fan speed for the duration.

Digital manual

There is a manual in the infotainment system under Apps. Owners say it isn't detailed.

Efficiency & performance Owner reports

  • Best consumption stories involve AC off, mild weather, smooth acceleration. One owner, coming from a hybrid, treated it like keeping the needle in the "green window". Another reported 17 kWh/100 km average.
  • Highway at speed with AC on and level-2 regen was another owner's "best" result (screenshot only).
  • 0–100 km/h: spec sheet about 13 s; owners recorded 10.3 s and 9.9 s (sport). The car eases off the line but is described as strong from 30 to 80 km/h.
  • Sport vs Eco: one owner prefers Eco in daily use.
  • Heavy-footed driving is addictive: "I'm struggling not to floor it all the time".

Other notes

  • Some Elite owners have switched off most of the ADAS features.
  • Camping mode lets you power TVs and other gear via V2L; see charging.
  • For the licence disc with smash-and-grab film, a member asked which holders people use; no answer yet.
