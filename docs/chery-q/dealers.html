Chery Q · Dealers

Dealer experiences

A province-level summary of what owners reported, with no dealerships, branches or staff named.

Why is this anonymous? The reports come from individual owners in a private chat. They are personal, unverified and can be out of date within weeks as staff and stock change. Naming a business on the strength of one person's account wouldn't be fair to the dealer or to the person publishing it. The patterns below are the useful part, and they don't need names. To find a good dealer near you, ask other owners in your area.
Ask in the owners' group →

Western Cape

Mixed
Several branches

Experiences varied widely between branches. Some owners described fast delivery, regular call-backs and friendly staff. Others reported slow replies after payment, paperwork and accessories arriving late, and collection dates that moved. Owners suggested comparing a few branches before committing.

Gauteng

Mostly positive
Several branches

Most reports were positive: quick handovers (one family collected two cars within about a day and a half), good after-sales replies, and in some cases a waiting list because of demand. One owner mentioned a branch they found difficult.

KwaZulu-Natal

Mostly positive
Several branches

Owners reported thorough handovers (one took about an hour and covered every menu and setting) and a dealer that offered to fetch buyers from home. One wall charger was couriered about five days after collection.

Other provinces

Positive
Limpopo, North West

Smaller-town branches were described as good, with clear communication. Out-of-province buyers noted they would pay for transport.

Patterns across the country

  • Wall chargers, logbooks and small accessories are the most common things to arrive late. See what the dealer doesn't tell you.
  • The individual salesperson often mattered more than the branch.
  • Smaller towns tended to get better reviews, though that was said half-jokingly.
  • Finance staff were described as both excellent and slow, depending on the branch.
  • Some members who post in the group are sellers advertising stock. Treat those as adverts, not reviews.
