Providers

Third parties mentioned by owners. Each comment is one owner's experience unless stated. Contact details are not published; ask in the group.

Charging networks and apps

GridCars (Charge Pocket app and RF card)
Public charging network, cashless payment

Card supplied with the car is linked to it. Reported working on BMW, Mercedes and Rubicon chargers. Newcomers were unsure whether other apps need loading too.

Rubicon
Public charging network

One owner confirmed the GridCars card worked on a Rubicon charger. No other feedback.

PlugShare
Charger map and trip planning

Recommended by owners for planning trips (e.g. Johannesburg to East London).

Home charging and power

Chery wall charger (via dealers)
7 kW home charger, Tellus Power app

Price and inclusion vary (free at several dealers, R7,499 at one). Owners say the app only helps to change amps and is awkward to register. Another owner says it can be integrated with Home Assistant.

Deye EV charger (sold via retailers and solar installers)
EV charger that connects to a Deye inverter; 7 kW single phase, 22 kW three phase

Owners with Deye inverters describe it as easier to install than a separate cable run to the DB. One owner charges from a 12 kW Deye inverter. Another owner says it is unnecessary with Home Assistant. Sunsynk makes a similar charger (mentioned only).

Electricians
Wall box installation

One Cape Town installation: R6,106.50 incl. VAT, 20 m run of 6 mm² cable, "working like a charm". The installer's details were an image and are not published.

Accessories

evcharge.co.za
Universal V2L adapter

Owner bought one and says it works great, up to about 3.3 kW.

Thule (Evo roof racks)
Roof racks, bike carriers

One owner carried bikes and reports no real effect on consumption (14 kWh/100 km).

Fireblock
Lithium fire extinguisher

One owner called it amazing but expensive.

Chery dealers
Key cover

One member says a new-model key cover is available at Chery dealerships.

Tyres

Palm Tyres
Tyre fitment (Kumho Ecsta)

One owner paid under R1,000 fitted. They have used Kumho for years and report the tyres "still going strong" at 5,137 km.

Wondergy (Aquila)
EV-specific tyre

One owner fitted these and shared the manufacturer's own road-test article. No ownership feedback yet.

Yokohama
Tyres, incl. EV tyres

One Comfort owner plans to return to them at replacement.
