Providers

Third parties mentioned by owners. Each comment is one owner's experience unless stated. Contact details are not published; ask in the group.

Charging networks, apps and route planning

PlugShare
Charger map and trip planning

Recommended by owners for planning trips. One owner says it shows some Johannesburg points that ChargePocket does not.

GridCars (ChargePocket app)
Public charging network, cashless payment, "Plan route" option

The RF card supplied with the car is linked to it (see Charging). One owner says its app does not show every charger (e.g. some SAEV ones) and advises cross-checking the charger operator's own app. Newcomers were unsure whether other apps also need loading.

Rubicon
Public charging network

The only feedback is that the GridCars card worked on one of its chargers.

Google Maps
EV charging filter that maps chargers along a route

One owner uses it for trips (tap "EV charging"); shared links to the planner did not work. A member notes it will also work through Android Auto once that arrives.

A Better Routeplanner
Route planner with charging stops

Mentioned by one owner among the apps in use. No feedback.

Home charging and power

Chery wall charger / Tellus Power app
7 kW home charger and its Bluetooth app

The app is mainly useful for changing current. One owner cannot get it to connect and it does not show the bound vehicle; it needs a registration step. Several owners only use the RF cards. Another owner says it can be integrated with Home Assistant.

Deye EV charger (via retailers and solar installers)
Connects to a Deye inverter; 7 kW single phase, 22 kW three phase

Owners with Deye inverters say it is easier to install than a separate cable run to the DB. Sunsynk makes a similar charger (mentioned only).

Victron EV Charging Station NS
Wall charger option for Victron systems

Suggested by one owner; no feedback.

Electricians and solar installers
Wall box installation

One Cape Town installation: "working like a charm" (price in Charging). A Centurion solar and electrical contractor was recommended as cheapest and neat. A Durban solar installer (Shift Solar) was recommended for charger and solar work. Contact details are not published.

Cables and V2L

evcharge.co.za
Type 2 charging cables and a universal V2L adapter

Several owners report the cables and adapter work, with "very high quality" and fast delivery (2 days, against a December wait elsewhere). Resellers exist at the same price. Prices in Charging.

Accessories and electronics

Hikvision D1 Pro dashcam (Takealot)
Dashcam, built-in wifi

One owner calls the install quick and neat, powered by USB-C. The R999 version includes a 64 GB card. Whether it records while parked was not confirmed.

AI box (about R1,200)
Runs Android apps such as Netflix on the car screen

One owner runs Netflix this way. One iPhone owner says it may also give Android Auto until the official update; setup details were promised. Unverified.

Wireless CarPlay / Android Auto adapter (R799)
Offered online

One owner asked whether it is worth it; no answer.

Temu and AliExpress
Key covers, AC-button covers, screen protectors, dashmat, mudflaps, key holders, rubber mats; one V2L adapter

Mostly positive. One key ring was too big for the key and the dashmat is one-size; one owner worries the single-piece dashmat will crack in heat. The AliExpress V2L adapter worked, with about R224 import duty on top.

Screen protectors
Fitting methods

One owner used leftover clear PPF with hand sanitiser and a squeegee; another used a purchased protector (wet method) and reports good results. Fitting tips from one owner: cool the car, switch the AC off, keep doors and windows closed, spray slip solution, align from the top, and lift a corner to adjust. A hairdryer on low heat loosens a misaligned protector.

Thule, Holdfast, Front Runner
Roof racks and rails

Reported to fit using hatchback-profile clasps and feet; one owner reused parts from an old Ford Fiesta hatch. Thule is expensive. One owner carried bikes on Thule Evo racks with no real effect on consumption (14 kWh/100 km).

Fireblock
Lithium fire extinguisher

One owner called it amazing but expensive.

Chery dealers
Key cover

One member says a new-model key cover is available.

Tyres

Palm Tyres
Tyre fitment (Kumho Ecsta)

One owner paid under R1,000 fitted. They have used Kumho for years and report the tyres "still going strong" at 5,137 km.

Wondergy (Aquila)
EV-specific tyre

One owner fitted these and shared the manufacturer's own road-test article. No ownership feedback yet.

Yokohama
Tyres, incl. EV tyres

One Comfort owner plans to return to them at replacement.

SmartTyre (Johannesburg)
Tyre retailer

One owner says they are working on a space-saver spare for the Q, with no ETA.

Insurance

Outsurance, Santam, Pineapple and brokers
Vehicle insurance

Owners shared quotes of R579–R1,200 per month with excess of R2,000–R6,000 (one Santam quote about R650 with R5,000 excess; one Pineapple quote R650 with R6,000 excess). Premiums vary with age, assets, accident record and years insured.

Styling

Pit Crew, Zwartkop Raceway (Centurion, Pretoria)
Vehicle wrapping

One member quotes R3,000 for a roof wrap in any colour and says they wrap all their vehicles. Another member liked a white Q with a black roof and plans to get a quote.
