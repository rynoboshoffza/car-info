Conflicting information

Topics where owners or sources disagree. Check your own car, documents or dealer.

Is the wall charger included?

  • Spec sheet: wall and portable chargers included on both trims.
  • Several owners: received free with the car.
  • One owner: paid R7,499 at a Chery dealer plus installation.
  • One owner: told it was part of a pre-order special.
  • Online reviews (second-hand): R12k extra.

Varies by dealerGet it in writing.

Vehicle weight

  • 1,380 kg kerb, 1,790 kg maximum (one owner's photo of the spec; leaves about 410 kg payload).
  • About 1,500 kg (a web search by one owner).
  • 1,680 kg and 1,700 kg (other owners).

UnresolvedThe figures may be different measures (kerb vs gross); the chat does not say. Check the vehicle plate or logbook.

HDMI input

  • The product page mentions HDMI integration.
  • One owner: an adapter such as the Carlinkit HD2CP is needed.
  • One owner: the manual says a USB to HDMI adapter will work.
  • One owner: there is no HDMI port; the product page mistranslated "HMI" (human machine interface), meaning the screen and voice system.
  • Two owners: the China-market car has an HDMI input and a video-in-motion option; SA cars do not.

Most reports say no portNo owner reports having used an adapter.

Do you need EV tyres?

  • Yes: quieter, lower rolling resistance, more range; normal tyres will not last with the torque.
  • Stronger sidewalls and noise-reducing inserts (several owners).
  • Kumho Ecsta (not sold as an EV tyre) fitted at under R1,000 and reported fine at 5,137 km.
  • Wear on other EVs is reported at both extremes: 15,000 km on fronts, 30–35,000 km on others.
  • One owner: keep the original tyres first and compare later.

UnresolvedNo Q-specific wear data yet.

Vehicle app: remote or Bluetooth only?

  • Several owners: works only within Bluetooth range.
  • Two owners: works without issues.
  • One owner: the car has its own SIM. Who activates it and what data it uses was not answered.
  • One owner: the car cannot connect to home WiFi.

UnresolvedOne owner asked whether the dealer linked the app to the key; it did for the owner with Bluetooth-only access.

Wall charger current and cable size

  • Amps: 32 A (maximum), 20 A and 16 A were each suggested.
  • Cable: 6 mm² with a roughly 40 A double-pole breaker, vs a 4 mm² cable rated 38 A on a 32 A charger.

UnresolvedDepends on run length and the existing board. Needs an electrician.

Deye EV charger vs Chery wall charger on solar

  • Deye charger: simpler install and connects to the inverter.
  • Chery wall charger with Home Assistant: no need for a more expensive charger.

OpinionDepends on your setup.

Owner's manual

  • An in-car manual exists under Apps.
  • Owners say the in-car manual is not comprehensive.
  • A fuller PDF manual was shared in the group.
  • Several owners had no manual or logbook after collection.

ClarifiedAsk the group for the PDF.

CarPlay connection

  • Needs manual reconnecting every time (several owners).
  • Now connects automatically, or during the drive (two owners).
  • Setting the phone as the primary device at setup fixed it (one owner).

Acceleration

  • Spec sheet: about 13 s to 100 km/h.
  • Measured: 10.3 s (unforced) and 9.9 s (sport).

Silver interior

  • Question whether silver is still only available with a light interior.
  • Two owners have silver with dark seats.
