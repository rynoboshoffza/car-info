Conflicting information

Topics where owners or sources disagree. Check your own car, documents or dealer.

Is the wall charger included?

  • Spec sheet: wall and portable chargers included on both trims.
  • Several owners: received free with the car, including at a Limpopo dealer (both chargers, sealant and card in a R389,000 deal) and two Free State dealers.
  • Some: free only because the car was a cancelled pre-order, or told only pre-orders get one. One owner is still waiting for a "free" charger the dealer lost.
  • One owner: paid R7,499 at a Chery dealer plus installation. Another was told Chery's own charger retails for about R9,000, and one resold an unused one for R6,000.
  • One owner: the dealer worked it into the price. Several owners: none included.
  • Two owners (hearsay): more likely if you take dealer finance. One owner: dealers tried to sell the free pre-order chargers to buyers.
  • Online reviews (second-hand): R12k extra.

Varies by dealerGet it in writing.

Android Auto: does it work, and when?

  • One owner: a dealer said it has worked since day one, but it does not.
  • A motoring reviewer's car appeared to show Android Auto; one owner says it is the CarPlay interface.
  • Timelines owners relayed from Chery SA and dealers: end of November (a dealer); "towards the end of this month" (a Monday call to Chery SA); end of December (a member on a call with Chery SA); "January" (a joke).
  • One owner says an AI box gives Android Auto (unverified; see Providers).

UnresolvedAsk Chery SA directly with your VIN.

Tyre pressure: 2.4, 2.5 or 2.7 bar?

  • Manual (a member quoting the PDF): 250 kPa (2.5 bar) cold on all four tyres for both wheel sizes; the low-pressure warning comes on below 200 kPa.
  • One owner expected 2.4; another reads 2.5 on the door-pillar placard (corrected from 2.3).
  • Two owners: at 2.5 bar the low-pressure warning stayed on; 2.7 stopped it. One owner at about 2.35 got pressure-difference errors.

UnresolvedMore in Tyres. Check pressures cold.

Tyre pressure monitoring: direct or indirect?

  • One owner, citing research and the brochure: indirect (wheel-speed based), with no temperature readings.
  • Several Elite owners and one test-drive car: per-tyre pressure and temperature are shown, which implies direct sensors. One owner says the brochure is wrong.
  • No Comfort owner has reported seeing temperatures; one owner says they have never seen it on a Comfort.
  • Foam sealant: one owner would avoid it because of sensors inside the tyres; another used it with no sensor problems.

Elite shows tempsComfort unconfirmed.

Warranty and service plan

  • One owner: a 7-year / 150,000 km cover applies to pre-order cars only, and may be a service plan rather than a warranty extension.
  • A dealer sent booklet scans showing a 5-year warranty.
  • One dealer: extensions are digitally linked to the first owner only.

UnresolvedAsk for written terms for the vehicle, battery and service plan.

Vehicle weight

  • 1,380 kg kerb, 1,790 kg maximum (one owner's photo of the spec; leaves about 410 kg payload).
  • About 1,500 kg (a web search by one owner).
  • 1,680 kg and 1,700 kg (other owners).

UnresolvedThe figures may be different measures (kerb vs gross); the chat does not say. Check the vehicle plate or logbook.

HDMI input

  • The product page mentions HDMI integration.
  • One owner: an adapter such as the Carlinkit HD2CP is needed.
  • One owner: the manual says a USB to HDMI adapter will work.
  • One owner: there is no HDMI port; the product page mistranslated "HMI" (human machine interface), meaning the screen and voice system.
  • Two owners: the China-market car has an HDMI input and a video-in-motion option; SA cars do not.

Most reports say no portNo owner reports having used an adapter.

Do you need EV tyres?

  • Yes: quieter, lower rolling resistance, more range; normal tyres will not last with the torque.
  • EV tyres have stronger sidewalls and noise-reducing inserts (several owners).
  • No: Kumho Ecsta, not sold as an EV tyre, is reported fine at 5,137 km.
  • One owner: keep the original tyres first and compare later.

UnresolvedNo Q-specific wear data yet.

Run-in period

  • A member's AI summary of the manual (low reliability): about 3,000 km, under 100 km/h for the first 1,000 km, re-torque wheel nuts after 800 km, new brakes 200 km.
  • About 6 owners ignored it. One says the text comes from petrol-car manuals. Two owners say nothing was said at delivery.

UnresolvedCheck the manual's own run-in section.

Battery charging habits

  • A member's AI summary of the manual (low reliability): charge to 100% weekly, recharge below 20%, prefer AC, stop DC around 90%, store at 40–60%.
  • One owner: avoid long periods at 0% or 100%, otherwise drive it and the BMS protects the battery.

UnresolvedCheck the manual pages cited (25, 35, 291).

Wall charger current and cable size

  • Current: one unit defaulted to 35 A rated and 40 A maximum; others cite about 32 A (7 kW) as the maximum. Owners set 30 A, 20 A, 16 A and 15 A.
  • Cable: 6 mm² with a roughly 40 A double-pole breaker, vs a 4 mm² cable rated 38 A on a 32 A charger.

UnresolvedDepends on run length, the existing board and your inverter. Needs an electrician.

Deye EV charger vs Chery wall charger on solar

  • Deye charger: simpler install and connects to the inverter.
  • Chery wall charger with Home Assistant: no need for a more expensive charger.

OpinionDepends on your setup.

Cruise control: 1 km/h steps
