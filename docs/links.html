Useful links

Tools and references that owners keep coming back to. Links open third-party sites; we don't control them.

Find & plan charging

PlugShare

Map of public chargers with user check-ins and photos. The app owners recommend most for trip planning (e.g. Jhb → East London).

GridCars (Charge Pocket app)

Cashless charging network and app. Owners load funds here and tap the RF card that came with the car; reported working on BMW, Mercedes and Rubicon chargers too.

Rubicon Charge

Another large public network. An owner confirmed the GridCars card worked on a Rubicon charger.

A Better Routeplanner

Route planner that works out charging stops for a given car. Not from the chatAdded as a common companion to PlugShare.

Home charging & power

Deye 22 kW Smart EV charger

Integrates directly with a Deye solar inverter. 7 kW on single phase, 22 kW on three phase (owner explanation).

Universal V2L adapter (evcharge.co.za)

Vehicle-to-load adapter. Not supplied with the Q; one owner bought this and reports it works great, up to about 3.3 kW.
