Replacement tyres reported

  • Kumho Ecsta fitted at Palm Tyres for under R1,000; fine after 5,137 km.
  • Wondergy EV tyres (Aquila), fitted by one owner. No ownership feedback yet.
  • Yokohama, which has EV tyres, is one Comfort owner's choice at replacement.
  • Prices mentioned: about R1,300 each in one report; Bridgestone about R2,500 each.
  • The original Comfort tyre brand is hard to find online.

Wear

  • No Q-specific wear data yet. Weight, instant torque and regen were cited as reasons for faster wear on EVs.
  • Other EVs: front tyres replaced at 15,000 km (heavy sport-mode driver) and 30–35,000 km (heavier car).
  • Some owners report wear similar to petrol cars.
  • One owner saves R500 a month for tyres.

Whether dedicated EV tyres are needed is disputed. See Conflicting info.

No spare

  • The Q has no spare wheel and no run-flats.
  • Run-flats were wished for by one owner; others say they ride harshly and need special rims.
