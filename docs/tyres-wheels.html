Tyre pressure

  • Manual: 250 kPa (2.5 bar, about 36 psi) cold on all four tyres, for both wheel sizes (a member quoting the PDF). The low-pressure warning comes on below 200 kPa. The placard is on the centre pillar of the left front door. Owners report different working pressures: see Conflicting info.
  • Check cold: before driving or after the car has stood for hours; driving raises the reading and tyre temperature.
  • At delivery: several cars were at about 3 bar (one owner: 3 bar at 21 °C, 2.97 and 2.51 after settling). One member says EVs are over-inflated to about 3.5 bar for shipping and should be let down at pre-delivery inspection (unverified).
  • Readings lag: after changing pressure the car needs about 1 km of driving to show the new value (two owners).
  • Warnings: several owners find the system very sensitive: pressure-difference errors every start (differences of about 0.01 bar) and continuous low-pressure warnings. Forecourt pumps are often inaccurate (one owner).
  • Whether the sensors are direct or indirect: see Conflicting info.

Alignment

  • One owner's car drifted out of its lane, the cruise control steered every 2 seconds and the tyres wore unevenly (left tyres on the outside edge, right on the inside) by 2,100 km. The dealer said alignment was needed and agreed to pay; alignment was probably not done at pre-delivery inspection.
  • A tyre shop said it did not have alignment settings for the Q, so owners advise having the dealer do it.
  • If you see lane-departure beeps on straight roads or uneven wear, check alignment early.

Prices

  • About R1,300 each in one report; under R1,000 fitted for Kumho at one retailer.
  • Bridgestone about R2,500 each.
  • A new alloy rim with tyre quoted at R4,500 (one owner says shop around).
  • Replacement brands owners chose: see Providers.

Wear

  • No Q-specific wear data yet. Weight, instant torque and regen were cited as reasons for faster wear on EVs.
  • Other EVs: front tyres replaced at 15,000 km (heavy sport-mode driver) and 30–35,000 km (heavier car).
  • Some owners report wear similar to petrol cars.
  • One owner saves R500 a month for tyres.
  • Whether dedicated EV tyres are needed is disputed: see Conflicting info.

No spare

  • The Q has no spare wheel and no run-flats. The starter pack includes a bottle of tyre sealant. One owner added a cordless compressor and other items.
  • One owner asked whether anyone got a space-saver spare; none was reported. A tyre retailer is reportedly working on one (see Providers).
  • Run-flats were wished for by one owner; others say they ride harshly and need special rims.
