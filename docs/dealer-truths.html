What the dealer doesn't tell you

Owner-reported surprises when buying and collecting. Get items 1–5 in writing on the handover document.

  1. Wall charger: included, bundled or paid depends on the dealer

    The spec sheet lists a wall charger and a portable charger as included. Owners report:

    • Several received it free with the car, plus the travel charger.
    • One paid R7,499 at a Chery dealer, plus installation.
    • One was told it was part of a pre-order special and got none; a car from a cancelled pre-order at another dealer came with one.
    • Others had it on back order for weeks, or couriered about 5 days after collection.
    • A post cites online reviews of an extra R12k charge (not confirmed by any owner).

    Ask: Is it included? If not, what is the price? Is it in stock, and what is the delivery date? Installation is separate (see Charging).

  2. Small accessories can be missing

    One buyer received an accessory pack with one car but not the other, and was told to collect the rest once plates were issued. Check the towing hook.

    Ask: Which accessories are standard on my trim, and when can I collect any that are missing?

  3. No V2L adapter

    The car supports vehicle-to-load (up to about 3.3 kW), but no adapter is supplied. See Charging and Providers.

  4. Own AC cable needed at many public chargers

    Chargers marked "SOCKET" and many BMW and Mercedes AC units have no cable. See Charging.

    Ask: Is a Type 2 cable included?

  5. Paperwork and manual

    • Several owners had no logbook after collection and emails went unanswered for days.
    • No printed manual reported. The in-car manual (Apps) is not comprehensive.
    • A fuller PDF owner's manual circulates in the owners' group; it is not hosted here.

    Ask: When will registration documents arrive, and who is the contact?

  6. Collection promises

    One buyer was told the car was at smash-and-grab film fitment and ready next day; the film had not been done and staff did not know who sold the car. Others report an unremovable windscreen mark and inconsistent Q-logo sticker positions.

    Ask: Inspect in daylight and photograph the car before signing.

  7. Trim and colour can change

    One pre-ordered Comfort was ordered incorrectly by the dealer; the buyer took an Elite or waited two months. Owners are waiting on beige and purple Elites.

  8. Transport is the buyer's cost

    Buying from a distant branch means paying for transport. One branch will fetch buyers from home.

  9. Finance deadlines

    One buyer's finance contact was unresponsive for weeks, then asked for a printed, signed document within 2 hours because it was month-end. Other buyers found finance staff helpful.

  10. Software and feature gaps

    • Camera failure at night: no fix yet, dealers report to Chery.
    • Wireless CarPlay needs manual reconnecting for some owners.
    • Android Auto: asked repeatedly, no answer.
    • Vehicle app works over Bluetooth only for several owners.
    • The product page mentions HDMI input; several owners say the car has none (Conflicting info).

    Ask: Demonstrate CarPlay and the vehicle app at handover. Ask the dealer to link the app to the car key.

Dealer experiences by province

Dealers and staff are not named. Each report is one owner's account, unverified and quickly out of date.

Western Cape Mixed

Varies by branch. Positive: fast delivery, regular call-backs. Negative: slow replies after payment, late paperwork and accessories, moved collection dates.

Gauteng Mostly positive

Quick handovers (two cars collected within about 1.5 days), good after-sales replies, a waiting list at one branch. One branch described as difficult. Wall charger given free at several.

KwaZulu-Natal Mostly positive

One-hour handover covering every menu. A dealer offers to fetch buyers from home. One wall charger was couriered 5 days after collection.

Other provinces Positive

Small-town branches described as good, with clear communication.

  • The individual salesperson mattered more than the branch.
  • Some group members who post stock are sellers; treat those as adverts.
